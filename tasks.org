** DONE t0032 Settle the repair/doctor naming and whether both should exist
=ort repair= and =pmgr doctor= are the same kind of command — check a thing,
report what is wrong — on two different subjects, under two unrelated names, and
neither repairs anything (=ortask.py:332=; its subparser help at
=ortask.py:528= still claims "find and fix ID problems"). The naming is now
decided; what remains is written down in =docs/projmgr.md= and =docs/ortask.md=
and staged below. Unblocks the question in =docs/logging.md= of whether these
findings are logged, and settles what =repair= promises before =t0004= and
=t0008= build on it.

Decisions, so nobody has to re-litigate them:

- Unify on =repair= in both tools. =doctor= survives as a deprecated alias for
  =repair --dry-run= — the read-only form, because =doctor= never wrote
  anything and the alias must not become a way to reach a command that does.
- A verb named =repair= should repair, but never behind your back: the bare form
  reports, then asks before each fix. =--force= skips the asking. Not =--fix=:
  =--force= is already this suite's word for "skip the safety" in =add= and
  =rm=. Without a TTY and without =--force=, refuse and exit 1 rather than hang
  on a prompt or silently decide to write.
- One exit rule for both tools: 0 clean, 2 when problems remain once the command
  finishes, 1 when the subject cannot be read. Today =ort repair= exits 0 with
  problems outstanding while =ort repair --dry-run= and =pmgr doctor= exit 2 —
  the same condition reported two ways.
- Add =info= to both tools, read-only, reporting what is *true*; anything that is
  *wrong* belongs in =repair=, so a script can rely on =info= having no opinion.
  =ort info= answers about a file, =pmgr info= about a project. =ort info=
  deliberately has no =--name=: the registry is the project layer's subject and
  two commands printing the same string leaves a caller guessing.
- The single-field forms (=--name=, =--path=, =--file=) are a scripting
  interface for prompts and window titles. One bare line on stdout, silence on
  stderr when there is no answer, mutually exclusive with each other and with
  =--format=. See "The single-field forms" in =docs/projmgr.md=.
